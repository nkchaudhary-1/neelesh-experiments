## What changed

First record. Discovered in the interim review. Eight commits, all on 2026-10-09, take a text-only workshop deck to a designed 13-slide deck.

## Why it changed

A workshop at Google DevFest 2026 (Google Developer Groups Noida), as stated on the title slide. The deck's argument is that a designer who can't really code can describe an idea to an AI and get something working.

## Design / UX changes

In order: colour-block themes, variable bold type and a kinetic intro; bold capital headings, halftone backgrounds and responsive text; a two-colour system (deep blue and off-white) with a calmer texture; a recoloured DevFest logo on every slide; a working Pomodoro timer slide after "Make a timer". Prompt cards carry a copy shortcut.

## Technical changes

One HTML file, about 340 KB, with fonts and logo embedded. It makes no network requests. Arrow-key navigation, a slide counter and a progress bar. A bug where prompt text vanished after the first few words was fixed in the last commit.

## Experiment insight

Tests whether a deck can demonstrate its own message: big type, two colours, prompts the audience can copy, and a live slide.
