I’m Neelesh Chaudhary, a senior product designer in Gurugram. I design interfaces, build experiments and explore ideas around technology, AI, interaction and digital products. This is where I document the things I’m currently curious about.

My day job is consumer product design. I have spent more than seven years on large B2C products across fashion, food delivery and health, and I’m currently at Nykaa Fashion, designing flows from onboarding and gamification to checkout and brand launches. Design systems run through all of it: I helped build Zomato’s Sushi system and I’m building Nykaa Fashion’s from scratch.

Everything here is unfinished by default. Some entries are live, some are still being built, and some are archived once they have taught me what they were going to teach me. Each one is a folder of Markdown in a repository, and publishing a new one is a commit.
